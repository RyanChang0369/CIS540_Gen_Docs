# Senior Residency for a Degree

## Final 30 Credits Policy

Students must complete their final 30 credits consecutively at UM-Dearborn. If
a required course for graduation is not offered, and an appropriate
substitution cannot be found, students may petition their college's Academic
Standards Committee for an exception to this policy. Students may also
petition for an exception to participate in a study abroad program. If an
exception is granted, students must still complete a minimum of 30 credits at
UM-Dearborn.
