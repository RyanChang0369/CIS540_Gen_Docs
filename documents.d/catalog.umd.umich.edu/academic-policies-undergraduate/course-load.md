# Course Load

## Course Load Policy

Students at the undergraduate level may elect a maximum of 18 credit hours in
a given semester. Students at the graduate level may elect a maximum of 12
credit hours in a 15-week semester and a maximum of 8 credit hours in a 7-week
semester. For graduate programs, individual colleges may impose a lower limit
than this campus wide policy. All students should contact their college for
policies and procedures regarding electing hours in excess of the maximum.
