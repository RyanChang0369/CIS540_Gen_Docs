# Student Location and Address

## Student Location and Address Policy

The University of Michigan Dearborn is able to determine student location for
purposes of conducting University business, contacting students in case of
emergency, and complying with Federal Regulations requirements related to
professional licensure or certification disclosures. This policy outlines the
procedures students are required to follow to verify their location and to
report changes in their location. This policy applies to undergraduate and
graduate students across all modalities of instruction.

The University of Michigan Dearborn uses a student's permanent address to
determine location. The permanent address is where a student permanently
resides and considers home. For students whose permanent address is outside of
any U.S. state or territory, their location will be considered the state of
Michigan. Students without a permanent address will be considered located in
Michigan.
